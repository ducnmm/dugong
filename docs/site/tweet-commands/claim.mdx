---
title: Claim
description: Claim reward campaign rewards or fallback market payouts.
---

Use this as a reply to a resolved reward campaign tweet or a resolved market
tweet.

```text
@DugongWallet claim
@DugongWallet claim reward
@DugongWallet claim payout
@DugongWallet claim winnings
@DugongWallet claim!
```

Behavior:

- For reward campaigns, the claimant must be a selected winner and the campaign must be resolved.
- Auto-creates the claimant account if needed.
- Credits the reward to the claimant's Dugong account and replies with a reward-claimed message and Suiscan transaction link.
- For markets, this is a fallback payout path when a resolved market has claimable payout coin types for the claimant. Market resolution normally pays winners automatically.
- If there is no claimable reward or payout, replies that there is nothing to claim.
