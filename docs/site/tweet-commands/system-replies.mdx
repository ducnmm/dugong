---
title: System Replies
description: User-facing success, error, and current gap behavior for tweet commands.
---

Dugong replies to command tweets after the processor reaches the reply step.
The reply is posted through TwitterAPI.io and is always a direct reply to the
command tweet that triggered the flow.

## Success Replies

- Account creation: welcomes the user and links the account creation transaction.
- Transfer: shows sent amount, sender, recipient, and transaction link.
- Market creation: explains how to bet and resolve.
- Bet placement: confirms the stake and side.
- Market resolution: summarizes the winning outcome and payout count.
- Reward campaign creation: shows reward amount, max winners, resolve instruction, and claim instruction.
- Reward campaign resolution: shows selected winner count.
- Reward claim: confirms the reward was credited.
- Market payout claim: confirms winnings were credited.

## User-Facing Error Or Status Replies

- Unsupported or unrecognized command, with a short explanation and command docs link.
- Account already exists.
- Market not found for the parent tweet.
- Market already closed.
- Market has no bets yet.
- Market is not resolved yet when a payout claim is attempted.
- Unauthorized market resolver.
- Reward campaign already exists.
- Reward campaign not found for the parent tweet.
- Reward campaign already resolved.
- Reward campaign is not resolved yet when a reward claim is attempted.
- Unauthorized campaign resolver.
- Reward already claimed.
- Nothing to claim.
- Generic command failure for tweet processing, transaction submission, account auto-create, winner search, or missing mirrored account state.

## Current Gaps

- Updating a handle is supported on-chain, but not currently exposed as a tweet command.
- Wallet linking is handled through the web app flow, not through tweets.
- Webhook delivery or queue failures that happen before Dugong has a tweet id may still only be logged.
